## Full description (itch.io)

Paste this into the itch.io description box, then apply the headings and bullets in its editor.

**It was supposed to be a closed test.**

One tester per region. Do not share this invitation. Late one night you log in to a game nobody has heard of, and the setup asks strange questions. What do you want most? What are you afraid of losing? Name the person you trust most.

Then the screen breaks.

You wake on cold stone at the foot of a tower. Someone with the name you just typed is shaking you awake and telling you that you fell asleep on watch again. They've known you your whole life. You have never seen them before.

Everyone here knows the legend: whoever reaches the top becomes a god. A window only you can see says you were selected. And the tower keeps showing you things from your own childhood.

**Tower of Recognition** is a story-driven, turn-based party RPG inspired by tower-climbing manhwa and classic menu-based RPGs. It's grim, but it's hopeful, and it's about the people you climb with.

### Climb

- **100 floors**, with stages that change from run to run and a boss guarding every tenth floor.
- **Classic turn-based combat** with four rows on each side. Where you stand decides who you can reach and who can reach you.
- **Learn what hurts them.** Eight elements and three weapon types. Land a weakness to stagger an enemy, and the floors before each boss teach you how to beat it.
- **Your wounds carry over.** HP and MP stay spent between battles, so every fight costs something.

### Build your party

- **Dozens of classes**, from your first job up through four tiers of advancement, plus hidden classes you only find by following clues through the tower.
- **A party that grows as you climb**, up to ten fighters at the top of the tower.
- **More than twenty recruits** with their own values and loyalty quests. Some will walk out if they hate what you've become.
- **Legendary demigods** from the world's myths, for those who earn them.

### Choose what you become

- **Hero, Villain or Vigilante.** Your choices shape your ending, who stands beside you and how the world treats you.
- **A childhood friend who never leaves you.** They stay whatever you do, so who they become is your doing.
- **Gods and devils from world mythology** compete for you. Every blessing comes with a curse, and refusing one can have a price of its own.

### A tower that lives without you

- **Guilds think for themselves.** They trade, raid, ally and go to war each time you climb a floor.
- **Join a guild, rob one, or found your own.** Claim territory, post your benched companions as captains, and rescue them if they're captured.
- **A real economy.** Prices move with supply, demand, wars and who rules the floor.

### Reach the top, and begin again

- **Become a god.** Conquer the tower, choose your domain and name your title.
- **Climb again in New Game+** with a new story. People pray to you, and you decide who gets answered.
- **Your legend carries on.** Past conquerors appear as gods in your other characters' runs.

### Play it your way

- **Four difficulties**, from a relaxed story mode to a true grind, plus an **Ironman** option on any of them: anyone who falls is gone, and if you fall, the run ends.
- **Rebindable keys**, adjustable interface size and colour themes.
- **Fully offline.** No account and no internet connection needed.
